Model and preprocessing provenance
==================================

gesture_v12_int8.tflite is the project's frozen v12 six-class model, copied
byte-for-byte from Build/deployment-v12-20260922/model. Its source checkpoint
and board-generated weight source hashes are recorded in manifest.json.

preprocess.dll is a Windows x64 build of this project's own gs_preprocess.c,
gs_preprocess.h, and preprocess_bridge.c included here for review. It does not
link the ST Edge AI host runtime. It was built with MinGW and imports Windows
KERNEL32 and Universal C Runtime APIs.

No ST host validation DLL, production.dll, ST inference runtime, or training
images are included here. The LiteRT interpreter is an external dependency;
its license and required notices must be supplied by the application packager.
This notice records provenance and does not grant rights beyond those held by
the project owner or the licenses of third-party runtime dependencies.
